April marked a major step forward across We Raise Hope programs. Bodgaun Primary Hospital treated 332 patients and completed its first successful lipoma and appendix operations, with emergency cases handled locally. Alongside that progress, 85 children enrolled in kindergarten, more than 60 children joined structured sports training, 298 students learned across four IT schools and 22 teachers received digital-systems training. These results reflect years of community leadership, donor support and investment in permanent local capacity.

---

At WE RAISE HOPE, we understand that healthcare is not confined to the walls of a hospital or clinic. We believe in reaching out to our community members where they live, work and play, ensuring that healthcare services are accessible to all, regardless of the circumstances. Through our comprehensive outreach program, we strive to address the diverse healthcare needs of our community and promote health and well-being at the grassroots level.

---

At WE RAISE HOPE, we are dedicated to implementing a holistic and innovative approach to healthcare management that prioritises quality, accessibility and patient-centred care. Through collaboration with prominent doctors, both Nepalese and international, and the implementation of cutting-edge technologies, we strive to revolutionize healthcare delivery in rural areas and ensure that every individual receives the attention they deserve.

Shree Setidevi IT School

"The school that started it all."

Established with the vision of our founder Alberto Luzzi, this was our first IT classroom, beginning with just one teacher and a few computers.

Today, over 70 students learn here each year, mastering essential computer skills that open doors to higher education and employment.

It's more than a classroom - it's a symbol of what belief and persistence can build.

For the first time in Bodgaun, patients who needed essential surgery received care without making the long journey to a distant operating room. Successful lipoma and appendix procedures showed what locally available surgical capacity can mean during urgent moments. Two surgeons supported the hospital during this milestone. Continued funding is needed for trained staff, supplies, maintenance and dependable operations.

Gravity-fed water systems bring clean water directly to households, eliminating the need for long treks to distant water sources. Our filtration units ensure water safety, while regular testing guarantees quality. Modern infrastructure replaces hours of daily labor with convenient, reliable access.
