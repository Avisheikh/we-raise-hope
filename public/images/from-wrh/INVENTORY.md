### April 2026 / program impact figures
- 332 patients at Bodgaun; first local lipoma & appendix surgeries; 2 surgeons
- 298 IT students across 4 schools; 22 teachers digital-systems trained
- 85 kindergarten enrollments; 60+ in sports training
- 70+ students/year at Shree Setidevi IT School; “over 400 children” computer literacy across three villages
- Other counters in JS: Students supported 340/480

### Mission / about
- `Hope is not a handout. It is a foundation.`
- `When healthcare, education, water and opportunity work together, families can build futures close to home.`
- Origin: 2015 earthquake volunteer response → long-term locally led work
- About nav sections: Our Mission · Where We Work · Our Connection to Nepal · Connecting With The US · Accountability · The People Behind The Work

### Programs (short)
**Healthcare / Bodgaun Primary Hospital** — five-bed facility; emergency, lab/X-ray, referrals, operating surgical room; outreach clinics, medicine kits, transport. CTAs: Sustain Hospital Care · Volunteer · Partner With Us. Headline: `The Surgical Room Is Ready. Help Sustain It.` / `Saving Lives, Restoring Hope`

**IT Education** — `WE HAVE THE SKILLS`; schools: Shree Setidevi (“The school that started it all”), Sipatindhara, Alex George Brown IT School (“Funded with hope. Built for the future.”)

**Kids Education & Meals** — nutrition, tutoring/reading circles, parent workshops; donate cause card `Kids Education and Meal`

**Clean Water** — gravity-fed taps, filtration, local maintenance, hygiene education

**Livelihoods** — `WE HAVE THE FUTURE`; sewing/woodworking/digital design; sports & arts; entrepreneurship
